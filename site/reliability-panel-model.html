Methods and limits

  • Outcome: utility-reported SAIDI excluding major-event days.
  • Ownership, state, year, and reporting-method indicators are included.
  • Each usable utility-year counts once; there is no customer weighting.
  • Uncertainty is clustered by utility.
  • Only source-audited exclusions are applied; high values are not automatically removed.
  • Weather is not included. The estimates are descriptive associations, not causal effects of ownership.

Excluding major-event days reduces the effect of exceptional storms, but ordinary weather, geography, vegetation, infrastructure, and reporting definitions can still affect SAIDI.
