Reviewed ownership changes

The figures beside each acquisition compare average prices before and after the ownership change, for the customer class selected above. They also show how prices changed over the same years at utilities that did not change ownership. All amounts use inflation-adjusted 2024 cents per kWh; averages give each year and each comparison utility equal weight. The acquisition year is left out. These descriptive comparisons do not establish causation.

Nine clean ownership-class changes occur between 2001 and 2024: eight from DOM to MTC and one from MTC to DOM. DOM means a U.S.-owned investor utility; MTC means a utility with a foreign or multinational parent.

Versant Power lineage (Bangor Hydro) · October 10, 2001 · DOM → MTC

Emera Inc. of Canada acquired Bangor Hydro-Electric, the utility lineage now shown as Versant Power. Because the acquisition closed in the first panel year, there is no full four-year pre-change window. Source

Niagara Mohawk · January 31, 2002 · DOM → MTC

National Grid plc of the United Kingdom acquired Niagara Mohawk. Only 2001 is available as a full pre-closing year, so this change does not have the planned four-year comparison window. Source

Green Mountain Power · April 12, 2007 · DOM → MTC

Gaz Metro Limited Partnership of Canada acquired Green Mountain Power; the legal utility and EIA identifier continued under the new parent. All three customer classes have four full years before and after. Source

Central Maine Power · September 16, 2008 · DOM → MTC

Iberdrola S.A. of Spain acquired Energy East, changing Central Maine Power's ultimate parent. Its published bundled prices cover as few as 27 residential customers and 6 commercial customers in the comparison years. These small groups do not provide a representative utility-wide comparison. Industrial prices lack sufficient consecutive years. Source

NYSEG · September 16, 2008 · DOM → MTC

The same Iberdrola–Energy East transaction moved NYSEG from a U.S. parent to a Spanish parent. Residential, commercial, and industrial prices each have four full years before and after the closing year. Source

Rochester Gas & Electric · September 16, 2008 · DOM → MTC

Rochester Gas & Electric changed parent in the Iberdrola acquisition of Energy East. All three customer classes have the planned four-year before-and-after price window. Source

Central Hudson · June 27, 2013 · DOM → MTC

Fortis Inc. of Canada acquired CH Energy Group, moving Central Hudson from a U.S. parent to a Canadian parent. The 2013 closing year is mixed; 2014 is the first full year under Fortis. Source

United Illuminating · December 16, 2015 · DOM → MTC

Iberdrola S.A. acquired UIL Holdings through Avangrid, changing United Illuminating from a U.S. parent to a Spanish parent. The first full post-closing year is 2016. Source

Rhode Island Energy · May 25, 2022 · MTC → DOM

PPL Corporation acquired Narragansett Electric from National Grid and rebranded the operation Rhode Island Energy. This is the one reviewed change from a multinational parent back to a U.S. parent; only two full post-closing years are available through 2024. Source
