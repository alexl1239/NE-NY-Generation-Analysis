Panel regression results

Preliminary pooled panel regressions using repeated utility-year observations from 2013–2024. The estimates are associations, not causal effects.

Specification

Priceit = ownershipit + market + year + reporting method [+ SAIDI or CAIDI] + errorit

  • Separate models for residential, commercial, and industrial bundled average prices.
  • Prices are inflation-adjusted to constant 2024 cents per kWh.
  • DOM is the ownership reference group; NYISO is the market reference group.
  • Routine SAIDI and routine CAIDI are added separately.
  • Each utility-year counts once. Standard errors are clustered by utility.
  • No customer weighting or automatic outlier deletion is used.

Reliability as the outcome

These regressions use routine SAIDI or CAIDI as the dependent variable and test whether reported reliability differs by ownership. Higher values indicate worse reported reliability.

Interpretation and limits

Across customer classes, the estimated MTC and COOP price coefficients remain below DOM after adding routine SAIDI or CAIDI. The reliability terms do not explain much of the ownership-price difference in these specifications.

These are pooled panel regressions, not utility fixed-effects estimates. Ownership, regulation, geography, infrastructure, and customer composition may be related to one another, so the coefficients should not be interpreted as causal. Only two utilities change ownership in the current period, which limits a within-utility causal design.

The price outcome is EIA bundled average revenue per kWh. Delivery-only prices or revenues are not included in these models. ROI/ROE is also not included.

Model input table
