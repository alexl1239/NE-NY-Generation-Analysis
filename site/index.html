Methodology & caveats

  • Data sources: PUDL's out_eia923__yearly_generation_fuel_combined table (EIA Form 923) for generation by fuel, joined to core_eia860__scd_plants (EIA Form 860) for each plant's balancing authority, queried via DuckDB.
  • Years: 2020–2024.
  • Utilities: filtered by exact EIA utility ID, not name matching, to avoid mixing in unrelated same-named subsidiaries (e.g. Con Edison's merchant generation arm).
  • Eversource: PUDL records this utility's generation under its older name, "NSTAR Electric Company" — the same legal entity, renamed here for continuity.
  • Limitation (utility-owned section): this shows generation reported directly under each utility's own name only. It does not allocate joint-ownership plants or roll up parent/subsidiary companies, so it is not the full generation mix delivered to retail customers — several of these utilities own little direct generation today and buy most of their power on the wholesale market.
  • Why the regional section exists: because of the above, the utility-owned charts alone don't explain these utilities' costs. The regional grid mix (NYISO / ISO-NE) reflects the wholesale market these utilities actually buy from, which is the more relevant dataset for a cost/revenue analysis.
